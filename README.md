# 🏋️ Prolift Fitness Gym Business Intelligence Dashboard

An interactive **Business Intelligence Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Excel, Power Query, and Power BI** to analyze gym operations, memberships, revenue, trainer performance, and business growth.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

The **Prolift Fitness Gym Business Intelligence Dashboard** is a business analytics project inspired by the operations of **Prolift Fitness Gym, Wadi, Nagpur**.

The objective of this project is to transform raw membership data into meaningful business insights that help evaluate the impact of the gym's expansion from **2,000 sq. ft. (2024)** to **6,000 sq. ft. (2025)**.

The dashboard enables management to monitor key performance indicators, identify growth opportunities, and make data-driven business decisions.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze membership growth from 2024 to 2025.
- Compare revenue before and after gym expansion.
- Identify the most popular membership plans.
- Analyze trainer performance.
- Understand customer acquisition sources.
- Monitor payment preferences.
- Evaluate Personal Training adoption.
- Track member demographics and engagement.

# 📈 Business Insights

- Gym expansion significantly increased member registrations.
- Revenue improved after increasing the gym area from **2,000 sq. ft.** to **6,000 sq. ft.**
- Monthly membership remained the most preferred subscription plan.
- UPI became the most commonly used payment method.
- Friend referrals and Instagram contributed to a large percentage of new memberships.
- Personal Training generated additional business revenue.
- Trainer performance varied based on member allocation.
